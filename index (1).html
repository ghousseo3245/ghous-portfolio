The problem

Why most business websites fail

A beautiful site that doesn’t load, doesn’t work on phones, or makes ordering hard will quietly cost you customers every day.

Slow & outdated

Heavy pages and old layouts make visitors leave before they see your products, especially on mobile data.

No WhatsApp ordering

Your customers already chat on WhatsApp. If they can’t order there in one tap, they’ll buy from someone who lets them.

Not mobile-friendly

Most buyers discover you on Instagram, on their phones. A site that pinches, zooms or breaks on mobile loses the sale instantly.

Your website isn’t a brochure. It’s your hardest-working salesperson. It should be built like one.

Live project · Leather goods · Ecommerce store

Tann & Thread

A premium online store for handcrafted leather goods, built to turn a chat-based business into a proper shop that takes orders around the clock.

The challenge

Selling through chats alone

Tann & Thread handcrafts leather bags, wallets, jackets, belts and shoes. Selling through chat messages alone meant there was no single place to show the full collection, take orders cleanly or keep track of stock. The brand needed a real store of its own, one that looked as crafted as the products.

The homepage had to do the heavy lifting in the first few seconds: signal premium quality, show the craft, and make the next step obvious.

The build

A complete store, not just a catalogue

A premium ecommerce store in the brand’s espresso and cognac palette: full product catalog with variants, cart and cash-on-delivery checkout, WhatsApp ordering on every product page, and an admin panel to manage inventory, orders and sales.

  • Product catalog, cart and cash-on-delivery checkout
  • Admin panel with inventory, orders and sales analytics
  • WhatsApp ordering built into every product page

The outcome

Open around the clock

The store is live and open around the clock. Customers browse and order whenever it suits them, and the client runs products, stock and orders from one dashboard. Open the live site and see it working.

Filters keep the full 15-piece collection easy to explore on mobile and desktop, so finding the right bag, shoe or jacket takes seconds, not scrolling.

Live project · Watches, bags & jewelry · Boutique website

Luxe Boutique

An elegant, gallery-style boutique for watches, bags and fine jewelry, designed to feel like stepping into a high-end store, not scrolling a catalogue.

The challenge

Premium pieces need a premium stage

Watches, handbags and fine jewelry sell on feel. A plain product grid makes premium pieces look ordinary. This boutique needed a storefront with the same polish as the pieces themselves.

The first screen had to create that in-store feeling immediately: calm, spacious, and unmistakably luxury, before a single product is seen.

The build

Curated like a gallery

An elegant, gallery-style website with curated collections for watches, bags and jewelry, refined serif type, generous spacing, and product presentation that treats every piece like it matters.

  • Curated collections for watches, bags and jewelry
  • Refined, gallery-style product presentation
  • Built to convert Instagram visitors into customers

The outcome

A boutique that keeps selling

A live boutique that feels like stepping into a high-end store, built to turn Instagram visitors into customers. Browse the collections exactly as a customer would.

Trust is handled all the way to the footer: a first-order newsletter offer, clear client-care links, and real contact details so hesitant visitors have a reason to stay in touch.
